# Demo Video Draft (3-5 Minutes)

## Part 1: Slide Pitch (1.5 Minutes)
- **[0:00 - 0:30] The Problem:** Explain that researchers waste 80% of their time organizing and extracting data, and traditional LLMs hallucinate citations, ruining academic credibility.
- **[0:30 - 1:00] The Solution:** Introduce Lumen. Explain the core value: Citation-safe RAG, Auto-Matrix extraction, and the 3-Layered Guardrail system.
- **[1:00 - 1:30] Value Proposition:** Highlight the impact: 10x faster literature reviews with 0% external hallucinations, keeping researchers in the flow.

## Part 2: Live Demo (2.5 - 3.5 Minutes)
- **[1:30 - 2:00] Project & Search:** 
  - Create a new project: "LLM Reasoning Capabilities".
  - Run an AI-suggested query.
  - Show the AI Screening score in action (saving a highly relevant paper, and showing the **Preventive Guardrail** modal if trying to save a bad paper).
- **[2:00 - 2:45] PDF Processing & Matrix:**
  - Show uploading a custom PDF.
  - Switch to the "Literature Matrix" tab. Click Generate.
  - Watch as Lumen automatically extracts Methodology, Key Findings, and Confidence. Show the **Detective Guardrail** highlighting a low-confidence row.
- **[2:45 - 3:30] Knowledge Map & Gaps:**
  - Briefly flash the Knowledge Map visualizing the connections between papers.
  - Navigate to Research Gaps to see what the current literature is missing.
- **[3:30 - 4:30] Report Generation & Export:**
  - Navigate to the Reports section.
  - Generate a literature review draft. Emphasize the **Protective Guardrail**—only saved project papers are cited.
  - Export to Markdown.
- **[4:30 - 5:00] Outro:**
  - Reiterate the Call to Action. "Lumen: Your AI Literature Review Assistant."
